# Charta Health (chartahealth.com): Company Profile, Product Portfolio, Business Model, and Revenue-Driving Product Line

Research date: 2026-10-19. Entity check: this is Charta Health, Inc., the San Francisco healthcare AI company that does AI chart review for providers and payers (chartahealth.com; investors Bain Capital Ventures, Madrona, SV Angel, South Park Commons, Refract). It is not Carta, Inc. (equity management) and not "CHARTA" in a GermanBroker.net merger listed on Tracxn. At least one aggregator (exa.ai websets) confuses Charta Health with Carta, so aggregator data needs care — [BCV/Hilaly search summary noting exa.ai confusion](https://websets.exa.ai/websets/directory/charta-funding); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/charta-health/__C9QTlpyvKqWcGjYr08MNoRtyNEWDCTrHLoHQBoieaag).

## Q1. What is Charta Health? Founding, founders, HQ, headcount, funding, valuation, revenue

### Takeaway
Charta Health was founded in 2023 by two former Rockset engineers, Justin Liu (CEO) and Scott Morris (CTO). It is based in San Francisco and has raised about $30M: an $8.1M seed (Feb/Mar 2025) and a $22M Series A (announced July 22, 2025), both led by Bain Capital Ventures. No valuation, ARR or revenue figure has been officially disclosed. I found no reported new round as of October 2026.

## Q2. Complete product/module list, Charta's naming, launch timing, and website navigation

### Takeaway
As of October 2026, chartahealth.com has two top-level audiences, "For Providers" (the home page) and "For Payers" (/payer). Providers get 7 "solutions": Autonomous coding, Revenue discovery, Payer compliance, Clinical quality, Risk adjustment, Clinical documentation improvement, Provider feedback. Payers get 6 "products": Retrospective risk adjustment, Automated second-pass & QA, Clinical chart intelligence, RADV & audit management, Prospective risk adjustment, HEDIS & quality analytics. All of them sit on one underlying AI chart-review platform. Charta has not published individual launch dates. The sequence below is reconstructed from press materials.

## Q3. Which product line drives the majority of revenue?

### Takeaway
No source states Charta's revenue split. The strongest circumstantial evidence points to provider-side pre-bill AI chart review as the majority revenue line: coding accuracy and revenue integrity (now branded Autonomous coding / Revenue discovery) together with documentation compliance audits (Payer compliance), sold to provider groups, FQHCs, urgent care, behavioral health and home health. The payer risk adjustment suite is newer, from 2025–26, and has thin public customer evidence. Confidence is moderate: the evidence is consistent but circumstantial, and the payer line could be growing quickly in contract size.

### Cited Findings
**Evidence that provider pre-bill chart review is core**
- The lead investor calls AI-driven pre-bill chart review "the first and core use case": 100% of charts reviewed before billing against each practice's coding and audit rules. BCV calls Charta "the leading compliance automation platform for provider groups" — [BCV blog, Jul 22 2025](https://baincapitalventures.com/insight/charting-a-new-era-how-charta-health-is-transforming-medical-chart-review/)

## Q4. Business model and pricing

### Takeaway
Charta sells B2B enterprise contracts. Its earliest documented model was outcome-linked ("providers pay only for approved findings," with "guaranteed ROI"). It has also signed "six-figure contracts." No list prices, per-chart rates or contingency percentages are public, and the current website has no pricing; all conversion goes through "Request a demo." Charta markets on ROI: 11% revenue uplift, 11:1 ROI, and chart review at "1/50th" the cost of manual review.

### Cited Findings
- At seed (Mar 2025), Pulse 2.0 reported that for Automated Pre-Bill Review "providers pay only for approved findings," and described "guaranteed ROI" without giving detail — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/charta-health-8-1-million-seed-secured-for-automating-chart-review-with-ai)
- The current homepage headline uses "guarantee": "What if you could guarantee revenue integrity and clinical compliance on every chart?" — [Charta homepage](https://www.chartahealth.com/)
- Early contracts were "several six-figure contracts" (annual value not specified) — [BCV blog](https://baincapitalventures.com/insight/charting-a-new-era-how-charta-health-is-transforming-medical-chart-review/)

Cost of manual chart review "1/50th" — [Charta About](https://www.chartahealth.com/about). Compliance audits "at 1% of traditional audit cost" — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/charta-health-8-1-million-seed-secured-for-automating-chart-review-with-ai). 10.6x chart coverage per FTE and a 72% accuracy improvement over internal teams — [Charta Series A post](https://www.chartahealth.com/resources/series-a)

Customized rules, a prototype for a customer in 6 days, and CPC/CRC-certified staff suggest services-heavy implementation. ### Gaps
- No public per-chart, per-encounter, per-provider, subscription or contingency-percentage pricing.
- I could not determine whether the "pay only for approved findings" model still applies in 2026. It appears only in one secondary article from March 2025.
